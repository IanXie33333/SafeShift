# WP1 Annual Establishment Patterns — Candidate Findings

## 1. Reporting establishment-years increased

Primary eligible warehouse establishment-years increased from 4,462 in 2016 to 9,860 in 2024, approximately 121%.

Reported DART cases increased from 20,191 to 59,454 over the same period.

This does not establish that workplace injury risk increased because reporting coverage and establishment composition may have changed.

## 2. Annual DART rates varied

From 2016 through 2024, primary-cohort DART rates ranged from approximately 2.73 to 4.34 cases per 200,000 hours, with the highest rate in 2021.

Rates are calculated from summed cases and summed hours, not averages of establishment-level rates.

## 3. Larger establishments had higher observed rates in the sensitivity scenario

| Year | Fewer than 50 employees | 1,000+ employees |
|---|---:|---:|
| 2023 | 2.468 | 5.283 |
| 2024 | 2.225 | 5.235 |
| 2025 | 2.308 | 4.511 |

These are descriptive exposure-sensitivity results, not evidence that establishment size causes higher injury rates.

## 4. One extreme hours record strongly affected the 2025 rate

One 2025 establishment-year reported 100,551,000,000 hours, accounting for approximately 97.02% of primary-cohort reported hours.

- Primary DART rate: 0.103
- Rate excluding only that record: 3.463
- Exposure-sensitivity DART rate: 3.658

The source record has not been silently changed or removed from the primary scenario.

## Limitations

DAFW and DJTR day totals remain blocked pending review of anomalous values. No findings based on day totals are approved.

All findings describe reporting establishments in the analyzed data, not necessarily the entire U.S. warehouse industry.
